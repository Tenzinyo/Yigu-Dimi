Yes, I am saying this while my AC is broken. I grew up in the South, which meant the scorching heat didn't determine whether I should go play soccer or not. In my house, we only had fans, and the noise of that rotating fan bothered me to the extent that I would go to sleep at 30 degrees without any cool air. And crazy enough, I had the best sleep. Whenever I was in a room filled with conditioned air, my placebo would tell me I was getting a headache because of this artificial air. Fast-track to 2026 when I am in the US of A, the only thing keeping me sane in the record-breaking summer heat is the AC. Maybe my body got too adapted to every little AC room from class to class, to stores, to even the bathroom. Now, even in 25 degrees without AC, I can't sleep, I can't study, I feel sleepy most afternoons, and all I want the landlord to do is to freaking fix that AC.
